# 🎤 Your 5-minute pitch

Every team hands in a short slide deck with its submission. The deck is **not scored**,
but the winners are only known after the overnight judging, so everyone prepares one:
**the winning team presents its deck at the award ceremony.**

## What to hand in
* `pitch_<team>.pdf` or `pitch_<team>.pptx`, **by 20:00**, posted in the organizers' Discord channel together with your notebook and `submission_<team>.json`
  (`<team>` as in your JSON file name, e.g. `pitch_Hardware_Heroes.pdf`).
* PDF or PowerPoint (`.pptx`), made with any tool (PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote, LaTeX, ...). PDF is the safest choice: fonts and layout look the same on the presentation laptop.
* Want a head start on the design? You can use the Qiskit Fall Fest slide template in this repository, `Qiskit_Fall-Fest_2026_Template.pptx`. It's optional.
* Hardware results came in after 20:00? Post an updated deck with them, until 21:00.

## Suggested structure (5–6 slides, ~1 minute each)
1. **Your cat in one slide.** Team name, members, and your headline numbers: simulator
   $F$ and error-reduction factor $R$, hardware $F$ on ibm_kingston, biggest certified $N$.
2. **What you changed.** Your recipe compared with the organizers' baseline cat (layout,
   cat shape, mitigation, ...). A circuit or chip-layout picture helps.
3. **Evidence.** Your "what helped and by how much" table from Mission 6, or one plot of it.
4. **Real hardware.** What happened on ibm_kingston, and how it differed from the simulator.
5. **What you learned / what you'd try next.**

## Tips
* Use the numbers from your submission, so the slides match what the judges scored.
* One idea per slide, big fonts, plots over tables of digits.
* No live demos: there's no time to run code on stage.
* Rehearse once with a timer. Five minutes goes fast.
